# Decisions

Follow [`factory/policy/decisions.md`](../../factory/policy/decisions.md).

When designing, research first, then **stop and ask** on gates. At most
five questions per stop. Wait for A/B (or a named third). Do not lock a
numbered `D` silently. BINDING is a human yes after `spec-reviewer`
returns `ready`. An agent may record explicit user approval under
`factory/policy/design.md` § User-authorized BINDING status without asking again.
